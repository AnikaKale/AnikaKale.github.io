        <h2>Project Goal</h2>
        <p>Design and integrate a robust chain-driven mechanism connecting a rotating mystic ball with an interactive Zoltar display, optimizing layout constraints to reduce friction and eliminate system stalling.</p>

        <div class="challenge-box">
            <h3>Engineering Challenges & Solutions</h3>
            <ul>
                <li><strong>Chain Alignment & Friction Control:</strong> Uneven sprocket spacing and incorrect dowel materials caused binding. Added physical spacers and refined mounting heights to minimize resistance.</li>
                <li><strong>Assembly Integration:</strong> Small alignment shifts immediately affected neighboring components. Adjusted clearancing and repositioned mounts to preserve smooth motion transfer.</li>
                <li><strong>Structural Stability:</strong> Standard hardware was insufficient for stable component mounting. Fabricated custom mounting plates on the plasma cutter to securely lock alignment.</li>
            </ul>
        </div>

        <h2>Reflection</h2>
        <p>Prototyping was essential to handle interdependent tolerances across multiple moving components. Managing timelines and tracking mechanical variations showed me that physical validation should happen alongside CAD modeling to discover alignment restrictions early.</p>
