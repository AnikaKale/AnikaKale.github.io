Project 06 Fabrication

Diwali & Christmas gift box

A laser-cut gift box combining Diwali and Christmas details. The project brought together a hinged enclosure, engraved surfaces, and hardware in 1/8-inch plywood, with iterations focused on panel fit and fastening.

Focus
Kerf, panel fit & hardware
Tools & methods
SolidWorks, laser cutting & engraving
Context
Fabrication project

Inside the project

01 The constraints 02 Design decisions 03 The outcome 04 What I learned

The constraints

  • Panel fit. Account for the material removed by the laser so the panels fit together.
  • Thin plywood. Attach the hardware without splitting the 1/8-inch stock.
  • Hinge clearance. Leave space for the lid and hardware to move through the intended opening.

Design decisions

Prototype the enclosure

Layout sketches and a cardboard model explored the panel arrangement, lid movement, and hinge placement before the final plywood pieces were cut.

Account for the laser kerf

Early panel fits were too loose. Kerf test cuts informed changes to the dimensional offsets in SolidWorks so the panels aligned more closely.

Prevent splitting during assembly

Directly inserting the hardware screws split the thin plywood. Pilot holes were added before the screws were installed.

The outcome

The completed box combined engraved panels with a hinged lid. The final enclosure incorporated the changes to panel fit and the fastening process.

What I learned

This project showed me the value of testing a joint before cutting a full set of panels. For another version, I would explore a wooden latch to reduce the need for metal hardware.
